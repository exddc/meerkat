Verdict

Lookout is the one that belongs in this app. It keeps the 2015 Meerkat silhouette (bulbous head, round side-ears, long thin neck) but drops the cute closed eyes and tan fills. Two open rings read as “watching you,” which matches a camera menu-bar utility better than Lucide’s tick-eyes or the original Meerkat smile.

Scout adds a foreshortened snout — more anatomically meerkat, slightly noisier at 16px. Gaze is the closest to Lucide Cat, but the tick-eyes stop looking at you. Menu is the 18px fallback if Lookout’s rings collapse.
